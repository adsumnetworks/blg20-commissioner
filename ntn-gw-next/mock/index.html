Set up a gateway

Seven steps, about five minutes. Stand next to the unit, with the SIM's details and your platform's device token at hand.

Step 1 of 7

1Find the unit

Pick the gateway you are standing next to. When asked, type its passkey.

2Where is it?

Every reading carries this position, and the satellite network needs it. Stand at the unit.

A typed position is written as "±25 m". Use the map's coordinates, not the street address.

3Name and key

The name is what your dashboard shows. The identity and key are how the platform knows this unit: the unit uses them to sign in.

Made from the name. Change it only if your platform gave you one.
A new 16-byte key is made on this phone. You see it once, then it lives only in the unit and in this phone's register.

4Network

How the unit sends its data. Pick what the SIM and the site allow. required

Keep both unless your operator supports only one.
Only if your SIM provider gave you one.
Satellite has a monthly allowance in kilobytes. The unit sends a short summary over satellite, not every reading.

5Platform

Where the data goes. The device token comes from your platform (ThingsBoard: the device's access token).

8 to 23 letters and digits. Written once and never shown again, not even to this page.

6Which sensors are yours?

The unit only stores sensors on its list. These are the ones it hears now. Untick any that are not yours. at least one

  • Door contactE8:02:…:9B
    −71 dBm
  • Unknown (a phone?)4F:…:11 rotating
    −80 dBm

7Check and finish

Read it over, then start the unit. It checks every part and tells you when you can leave.
